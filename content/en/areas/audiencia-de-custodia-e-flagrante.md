## The first 24 hours decide a great deal

After an arrest in the act (*prisão em flagrante*), the law requires the person to be brought before a judge within 24 hours — the so-called custody hearing. At that point the judge decides whether the arrest will be set aside, converted into pre-trial detention or replaced by alternative measures.

It is one of the most decisive moments of the entire criminal case. Having a prepared lawyer at your side can be what secures your release.

### What is at stake at the custody hearing

- Review of the legality of the arrest.
- Investigation of any abuse or violence during the arrest.
- Decision on provisional release, bail or alternative measures.
- Whether or not the arrest is converted into pre-trial detention.

> **Time is everything.** The sooner the firm is called, the greater its ability to gather documents, guarantees and arguments for the hearing.

::: faq
? What is a custody hearing?
It is the presentation of the arrested person to a judge within 24 hours of the arrest, to assess the legality of the arrest and decide on release or alternative measures.

? Can I be released at the hearing itself?
Yes. The custody hearing can result in the arrest being set aside, provisional release or alternative measures, depending on the case and on the work of the defense.

? Do I need a lawyer even if a public defender is available?
A trusted lawyer dedicated exclusively to your case can gather specific evidence and arguments and give your release their full attention.

? How much time do I have to act?
None to spare. Since the hearing takes place within 24 hours, contact should be made as quickly as possible so the defense can be prepared.
