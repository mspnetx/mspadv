## When a family crosses borders

Marriages between people of different nationalities, couples living in different countries, children with dual citizenship, assets spread around the world: in these cases, before discussing the merits, you have to answer questions that never arise in an ordinary case. Which country can hear the case? Which law applies? Will the decision be valid on the other side?

Getting those answers wrong means obtaining a decision that cannot be enforced where it matters. That is why the international strategy must be defined from the very first step.

### Situations we handle

- Divorce in Brazil when one spouse is a foreigner or lives abroad.
- Recognition in Brazil of a divorce granted in another country.
- Recognition of foreign judgments before the Superior Court of Justice (STJ).
- Division of assets located in Brazil in foreign divorces.
- Custody disputes and international relocation of children.
- International child abduction cases (Hague Convention).
- Cross-border recovery of support.

> A simple uncontested divorce granted abroad can, in many cases, be registered directly at the Brazilian civil registry, with no proceedings before the STJ. When it also deals with custody, support or property, recognition by the court is still required.

::: faq
? I am a foreigner and got married in Brazil. Can I divorce there while living abroad?
As a rule, yes. The divorce can be handled under a power of attorney, without travelling to Brazil, especially when both spouses agree.

? Is my foreign divorce valid in Brazil?
It has to be recognized. A simple uncontested divorce can be registered directly at the civil registry; when it also covers custody, support or property, it depends on recognition by the STJ.

? Can a foreign court decide on real estate located in Brazil?
In a divorce, the division of assets located in Brazil falls under the exclusive jurisdiction of the Brazilian courts. For that reason, separate proceedings in Brazil are often required.

? My child was taken to another country without my consent. What should I do?
Act immediately. The Hague Convention provides a procedure for the child's return that is most effective when started quickly.
:::
