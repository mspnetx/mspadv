## The most powerful tool against unlawful imprisonment

Habeas corpus is the constitutional guarantee that protects freedom of movement against illegality or abuse of power. It is the proper remedy when someone suffers — or is threatened with — unlawful imprisonment or coercion.

Speed is essential: a well-reasoned petition filed at the right moment can secure release within hours, including through a request for interim relief.

### When habeas corpus applies

- Pre-trial detention without adequate grounds.
- Detention beyond a reasonable time.
- Unlawful arrest or arrest based on tainted evidence.
- Dismissal of an abusive investigation or prosecution.
- Unlawful coercion during investigations.

> Habeas corpus can be filed at any time, and even preventively, when there is a concrete threat to freedom.

## How we work

We analyze the illegality urgently, draft the petition with a request for interim relief and file it with the competent court, closely following the decision and taking any further steps required.

::: faq
? What is habeas corpus?
It is the constitutional action that protects freedom of movement against unlawful imprisonment, the threat of it, or abuse of power.

? How long does a decision take?
With a request for interim relief, a decision can come within hours or days, depending on the court and on the urgency shown in the petition.

? Is habeas corpus only for people already in prison?
No. Preventive habeas corpus is available when there is a concrete and imminent threat to freedom, even before an arrest.

? Can I file a habeas corpus petition during an investigation?
Yes. It can be used to end investigations or prosecutions that are plainly abusive or lack legal grounds.
:::
