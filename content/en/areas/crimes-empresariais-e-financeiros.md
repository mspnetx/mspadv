## When criminal risk reaches the business world

Business owners and executives may face criminal accusations arising from their business activity — often amid complex transactions, accounting reports and investigations run by specialized agencies.

These cases demand a highly technical defense, able to work with accounting records, financial flows and economic criminal law, always with the discretion the client's reputation requires.

### Areas we cover

- Crimes against the national financial system.
- Money laundering and concealment of assets.
- Crimes against the economic and tax order.
- Fraud and fraudulent management.
- Corporate crimes and the liability of directors and officers.

> In white-collar cases, the defense ideally begins before charges are filed: at the investigation stage, when it is still possible to influence the course of the case.

## How we work

We act from the investigation stage, analyzing accounting and financial records, building the defense, attending statements and operations, and protecting the client's assets and reputation in every court.
