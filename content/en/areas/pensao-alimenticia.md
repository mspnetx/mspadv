## A fair amount depends on evidence, not on a formula

Brazilian law sets no fixed percentage for support. The amount is defined by the balance between the needs of the person receiving it and the means of the person paying — and that balance is shown with documents, not assumptions.

That is why both the person claiming and the person asked to pay need a well-prepared case: actual expenses, standard of living, formal and informal income, other children and obligations. A poorly set amount creates years of conflict.

### Situations we handle

- Setting child support, including interim support at the start of the case.
- Support during pregnancy.
- Support between former spouses and partners, where applicable.
- Review of the amount after a change in income or needs.
- Termination, when the obligation is no longer justified.
- Enforcement of unpaid support and defense of the person being charged.

> Support debt is the only debt that can lead to civil imprisonment in Brazil. If you have been served with an order to pay within three days, seek advice immediately.

::: faq
? Is support always 30% of the salary?
No. That figure is only a custom. The amount is set case by case, according to the needs of the recipient and the means of the payer.

? Does child support end automatically when the child turns 18?
No. Termination must be requested in court. Support is generally maintained while the child is studying and not yet able to support themselves.

? I lost my job. Can I simply pay less?
Not on your own initiative. Until a court decision or approved agreement changes the amount, the obligation remains the same and the difference accumulates as debt. The proper route is a review claim.

? Can I be arrested for owing support?
Yes. Civil imprisonment may be ordered for non-payment of the most recent instalments. With prompt advice, it is possible to pay, justify or negotiate before that happens.
:::
