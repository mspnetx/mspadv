## The defense does not end with the judgment

Sentence enforcement is the stage at which the sentence is actually served — and where many rights can be won or, unfortunately, lost for lack of proper legal follow-up.

Benefits such as regime progression, parole and sentence reduction depend on well-reasoned requests and on meeting legal requirements. Careful follow-up can significantly reduce time spent in prison.

### Rights and benefits we work on

- Regime progression (from closed to semi-open and open conditions).
- Parole.
- Sentence reduction through work and study.
- Pardon and commutation of sentences.
- Temporary leave and other benefits.

> Many benefits are not granted automatically. They must be requested at the right time, with the proper documentation.

## How we work

We monitor the sentence calculation, identify the benefits available at each stage, file well-grounded requests and appeal when rights are wrongly denied.

::: servicos
- **Regime progression** | Requests to move from closed conditions to less severe regimes.
- **Parole** | Review of the requirements and application for the benefit.
- **Sentence reduction** | Reduction of the sentence through work and study.
- **Disciplinary proceedings** | Defense against sanctions that affect benefits.
:::

::: faq
? When am I entitled to regime progression?
Progression depends on serving a fraction of the sentence and on legal requirements that vary with the offense. An analysis of the case defines the right moment to apply.

? What is sentence reduction (*remição*)?
It is the reduction of time to be served through work or study. For every three days worked, for example, one day of the sentence is deducted.

? Does a disciplinary offense affect benefits?
Yes. Serious offenses can interrupt time periods and harm benefits. A defense can be presented against such sanctions.

? Are benefits granted automatically?
No. Most depend on a reasoned request. Legal follow-up ensures that no right is lost for want of an application.
:::
