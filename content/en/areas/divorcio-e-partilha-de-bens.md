## Ending a marriage takes more than signing papers

Divorce ends the marriage bond, but it opens a series of decisions that stay with you for years: the division of property, the home, custody of the children, support and even the use of a surname. Decisions made in a hurry or without advice tend to be costly later.

In Brazil there is no need to prove fault or wait through a separation period: the wish of one spouse is enough. What changes from case to case is the route — and choosing the right one makes all the difference in time, cost and stress.

### Possible routes

- **Uncontested divorce at a notary office**, when there is agreement on every point — usually the fastest route.
- **Uncontested divorce in court**, when the agreement must be approved by a judge.
- **Contested divorce**, when there is no agreement on property, children or support.
- **Dissolution of a stable union**, which raises the same property and family questions.

> The divorce itself can be granted early in the proceedings, while property, custody and support remain under discussion. No one is required to stay married while waiting for the assets to be divided.

## How we work

We start with an assessment of the assets and of the marital property regime: what is shared, what is separate property, and how to treat companies, mortgaged real estate, investments and debts. From there we seek an agreement whenever it protects the client — and litigate firmly when necessary, including urgent measures to prevent assets from being dissipated.

::: faq
? How long does a divorce take in Brazil?
It depends on the route. An uncontested divorce at a notary office can be completed within days once the documents are gathered. A contested divorce varies with the complexity of the assets and of the issues involving children.

? Can I get divorced without my spouse's consent?
Yes. Divorce is a right of the person requesting it; the other spouse cannot prevent it. Disagreement can only concern property, custody or support, which the judge will decide.

? How are assets divided?
It depends on the marital property regime. Under partial community of property — the most common regime — assets acquired for value during the marriage are generally shared, while inheritances, gifts and pre-marital assets are not.

? Do I have to leave the family home to file for divorce?
No. Whether to stay or leave should be assessed case by case, since it can have practical effects on custody and on the use of the property. In situations of risk, specific court measures are available.
:::
