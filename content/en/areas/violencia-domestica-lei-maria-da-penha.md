## A sensitive field that calls for technical defense and balance

Proceedings under the Maria da Penha Law (Law No. 11,340/2006) follow their own procedure and are highly sensitive. Accusations can arise suddenly, with urgent protective orders applied immediately, removing the person under investigation from the home, from the children and from family life.

In these cases the defense must act quickly, skilfully and responsibly — always respecting the seriousness of the subject, while securing the constitutional right to a full defense for the person accused.

### Situations we handle

- Imposition or review of urgent protective orders.
- Accusations of threat, bodily injury, insult and breach of a protective order.
- Trial hearings before the Domestic Violence Court.
- Cases involving unfounded or disproportionate accusations.

> Every case is assessed individually. Our commitment is to a technical defense, conducted seriously and with respect for everyone involved.

## How we work

We assess the evidence, the context and the proportionality of the measures imposed, build the most suitable defense and accompany the client at every hearing, seeking the best possible outcome within the strategy defined.

::: faq
? Can protective orders be reviewed?
Yes. Protective orders can be reviewed at any time when new elements are presented. The defense can show that a measure is unnecessary or disproportionate.

? Does every accusation lead to a conviction?
No. The accusation must be proven. A technical defense examines evidence, statements and context to show inconsistencies in the accusation where they exist.

? Are the proceedings confidential?
Cases of this nature are handled with caution, and the firm's service is conducted in absolute confidence.

? I breached a protective order. What can happen?
A breach can be a separate criminal offense and lead to arrest. Immediate legal advice is essential to keep the situation from getting worse.
:::
