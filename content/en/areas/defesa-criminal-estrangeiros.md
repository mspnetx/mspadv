## Facing the criminal justice system in a foreign country

Being arrested or investigated abroad is one of the most stressful experiences anyone can face. The language barrier, unfamiliar Brazilian laws and distance from family make everything harder — and more urgent.

MSPADV provides full criminal defense for foreigners in Brazil, with fluent communication in **English and Portuguese**. We make sure you understand every step and make decisions with full awareness.

### Common difficulties foreigners face

- Language barriers during statements, hearings and paperwork.
- Risk of pre-trial detention wrongly based on foreign nationality.
- Lack of knowledge of basic rights guaranteed by the Brazilian Constitution.
- Impact of the criminal case on immigration status and visas.
- Difficulty contacting family and the consulate.

> **Important:** the Brazilian Constitution guarantees foreigners the same rights as Brazilian citizens in criminal proceedings, including an interpreter and a full defense.
