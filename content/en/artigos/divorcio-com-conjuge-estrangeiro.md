Marriages between Brazilians and foreigners are increasingly common — and when they end, they raise questions an ordinary divorce does not. Understanding the basics helps you avoid decisions that later cannot be enforced.

## Can I get divorced in Brazil?

As a rule, yes: when the couple lives in Brazil, when the spouse to be served lives there, or when both agree to settle the matter in the country. Where there is agreement, the divorce can even be completed at a notary office, with the spouse who is abroad represented under a power of attorney.

## What if the divorce was already granted in another country?

It must be recognized to take effect in Brazil. There are two routes:

- **Direct registration at the civil registry**, for a simple uncontested divorce that only dissolves the marriage.
- **Recognition by the Superior Court of Justice (STJ)**, when the decision also deals with custody, support or division of property.

## Assets in Brazil follow their own rule

The division of assets located in Brazil is a matter for the Brazilian courts. Someone who divorced abroad and owns real estate in Brazil may need specific proceedings there to formalize the division.

## Children and moving abroad

Neither parent may move a child to another country without the other's consent or a court order. An irregular removal may amount to international child abduction, with a request for immediate return under the Hague Convention.
