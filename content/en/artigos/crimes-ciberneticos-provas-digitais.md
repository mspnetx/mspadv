As cybercrime grows, so does the number of accusations based on digital evidence. But this evidence, often seen as infallible, can contain significant flaws.

## An IP address does not identify a person

An IP address points to an internet access point, not to whoever actually carried out a given action. Shared networks, compromised devices and VPNs make that link fragile.

## The chain of custody of digital evidence

Digital evidence must be collected and preserved under strict procedures. Failures in the chain of custody can undermine the validity of the evidence as a whole.

## A technical defense makes the difference

Critically examining forensic reports, records and how the evidence was obtained is fundamental. Many accusations rest on mistaken readings of technical data.
