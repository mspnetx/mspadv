The custody hearing is one of the most important safeguards in Brazilian criminal procedure. It requires every person arrested to be brought before a judge within 24 hours of the arrest.

## What is the purpose of the custody hearing?

The purpose is threefold: to check the legality of the arrest, to investigate any abuse or violence during it, and to decide whether the person needs to remain in custody.

## What can the judge decide?

- **Set the arrest aside**, when it was unlawful.
- **Grant provisional release**, with or without bail.
- **Impose alternative measures** other than imprisonment.
- **Order pre-trial detention**, when the legal requirements are met.

## The role of the defense

The lawyer can present documents, show the client's ties to the community, argue that detention is unnecessary and point out illegalities. It is a moment of intense, strategic work.
