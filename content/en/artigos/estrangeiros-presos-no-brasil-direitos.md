Being arrested in a foreign country is a frightening experience. But it is important to know this: the Brazilian Constitution guarantees foreigners the same rights as Brazilian citizens in criminal proceedings.

## Guaranteed rights

- The right to an interpreter in statements and hearings.
- The right to remain silent and to a full defense.
- The right to have your country's consulate notified of the arrest.
- The right to remain free during the proceedings, where applicable.

## Nationality does not justify detention

A common mistake is to believe that a foreigner must remain in custody because of a "flight risk". The courts have been clear: being a foreigner does not, by itself, justify pre-trial detention.
