A sentence is not static. While serving it, a convicted person can earn benefits that make the conditions less severe — among them, regime progression.

## What is regime progression?

It is the move from a more severe regime to a milder one: from closed to semi-open conditions, and from semi-open to open. It depends on serving a fraction of the sentence and on legal requirements.

## What are the requirements?

- Serving part of the sentence (the fraction varies with the offense and the person's record).
- Good conduct in prison.
- No serious disciplinary offenses that interrupt the counting of time.

## Why follow-up matters

Benefits are not granted automatically. They must be requested at the right time, with proper documentation. Careful follow-up prevents the person from serving more time than is due.
