Provided for in the Constitution, habeas corpus protects freedom of movement against any illegality or abuse of power. It is one of the most powerful instruments in Brazilian law.

## When does habeas corpus apply?

- Pre-trial detention without adequate grounds.
- Detention beyond a reasonable time.
- Imprisonment based on unlawful evidence.
- An investigation or prosecution without legal grounds.

## What is a request for interim relief?

Interim relief (*liminar*) is an urgent decision that can secure release even before the final ruling on the petition. When well reasoned, it can take effect within hours.

## Preventive habeas corpus

When there is a concrete threat of arrest, a preventive petition can be filed to obtain a safe-conduct that protects the person in advance.
