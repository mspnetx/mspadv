An arrest is a moment of panic — and that is exactly when most mistakes are made. The first hours are decisive, and a few simple steps can make an enormous difference to the outcome of the case.

## 1. Use your right to remain silent

You have a constitutional right to remain silent. Nothing you say without legal advice will help — and it may hurt. State that you wish to speak to a lawyer before making any statement.

## 2. Call a lawyer immediately

Time is the most important factor. After an arrest, the law requires a custody hearing within 24 hours. The sooner the defense is called, the better the chance of gathering arguments and documents to secure release.

## 3. The custody hearing is the first major moment

At this hearing the judge decides whether the arrest will be set aside, converted into pre-trial detention or replaced by alternative measures. The presence of a prepared lawyer is decisive.

> If a family member has been arrested, do not wait. Gather their personal documents and seek legal advice immediately.

## 4. Do not try to "sort it out" yourself

Conversations with police officers, promises or improvised explanations usually make things worse. A technical defense exists precisely to handle this moment strategically.
