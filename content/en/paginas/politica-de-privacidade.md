*Last updated: November 2026.*

MSPADV — Marcelo Paiva Advocacia respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data, in compliance with the Brazilian General Data Protection Law (Law No. 13,709/2018 — LGPD).

## 1. What data we collect

We collect only the data you voluntarily provide through the contact forms and service channels, such as your name, phone number, email and the description of your case.

## 2. How we use your data

- To respond to your contact and consultation requests.
- To provide legal services, when engaged.
- To comply with legal and regulatory obligations.

## 3. Sharing

Your data is not sold or shared with third parties for commercial purposes. Attorney–client confidentiality is absolute and protected by law.

## 4. Storage and security

We adopt technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, loss or misuse.

## 5. Your rights

You may at any time request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal data, as well as information on how it is processed, by email at [mspadv.rj@gmail.com](mailto:mspadv.rj@gmail.com).

## 6. Cookies and local storage

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. It stores only your language preference in your own browser.

## 7. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at [mspadv.rj@gmail.com](mailto:mspadv.rj@gmail.com).
